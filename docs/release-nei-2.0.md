# Ship of Harkinian Android — NEI 2.0

## Downloads

**For most players, choose Regular SOH.** It most closely follows the standard Ship of Harkinian experience. The other APKs are alternative forks for players who specifically want their changes.

- **Regular SOH — v9.2.3-android.14:** Android port of [Harbour Masters' Ship of Harkinian](https://github.com/HarbourMasters/Shipwright). Unchanged from the previous release.
- **Cel-Shading Fork — v9.2.3-celshade0.5-android.6:** Android port of [Roborich's cel-shading fork](https://github.com/roborich/Shipwright). Unchanged from the previous release.
- **NEI — 2.0:** Updated Android port of [Skijer's Not Enough Items fork](https://github.com/skijer/Shipwright), based on the newer NEI 2.0 codebase.

These three apps use separate Android app IDs and can be installed together. Fusion is no longer included or maintained starting with this release.

## NEI 2.0 changes

- Updates to the newer upstream NEI codebase and its expanded custom-item systems.
- Improves custom item, equipment, and mask icons and name labels, including new higher-resolution artwork for selected items.
- Supports optional import of selected HD mask and equipment icons from compatible packs already installed in 2Ship2Harkinian, including the Gilded Sword and Fierce Deity sword icons.
- Includes Android menu, controller, touch-control, and data-folder support.
- Addresses identified Save Editor crashes and a texture-loading assertion encountered with replacement model materials.

## Installation notes

- Back up your saves and settings before updating. Compatibility with every older NEI save and third-party mod has not been verified.
- Supply your own supported game data. ROMs and third-party graphics packs are not included.
- HD graphics-pack imports are optional and require compatible source files. Enable Alternate Assets to use imported replacements.
- The first launch may prepare updated support files. Allow preparation to finish before closing the app.

Only NEI changes version in this release. Regular SOH and Cel Shading retain their existing APKs and version numbers.
